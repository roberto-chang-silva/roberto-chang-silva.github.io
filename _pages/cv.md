Research Experience
======
* **Ph.D. Researcher (Applied AI)**, Seoul National University of Science and Technology, Seoul, KR (Sep 2023 -- Present)
  * Supervisor: Prof. SeongYoung Park, [AIRLab](https://air.seoultech.ac.kr/index.do)
  * Developing a Transformer-based model for flash drought prediction, outperforming both LSTM and CNN baselines in spatiotemporal forecasting accuracy across surface and root zone soil moisture variables.
  * Designed a novel satellite-informed multi-criteria sensor placement algorithm (NSGA-II) for urban PM2.5 monitoring, optimizing sensor distribution while reducing deployment costs and maintaining near-optimal spatial coverage.

* **M.Sc. Researcher**, Kyung Hee University (EMSEL Lab), Yongin, KR (Mar 2021 -- Aug 2023)
  * Supervisor: Prof. ChangKyoo Yoo
  * Architected a Spatiotemporal Long Short-Term Graph Convolutional Network (LSTGraphNet) for urban PM2.5 forecasting, improving prediction performance by **22%** over baseline models and reducing underestimation at high temporal horizons by **10%**.
  * Demonstrated robust generalization of LSTGraphNet at monitoring stations with low spatiotemporal correlation, a historically challenging scenario for graph-based air quality models.
