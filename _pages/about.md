This is my corner of the web. Here I share things I’m working on, thinking about, or just find interesting; from climate and data science to systems, containers, and whatever else catches my attention.

Nothing here is polished for perfection; it’s shared as-is because it helps me think, learn, and keep track of ideas. If something here is useful to you, that’s a bonus.

I’m a researcher by training, a tinkerer by habit, and I like building tools and workflows that make complex problems a bit easier to handle.
