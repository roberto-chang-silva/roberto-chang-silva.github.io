The other day I was interested on creating a big list of trivia questions to play with my friends. I surfed the internet and found these!

## Datasets

- **[mandarjoshi/trivia_qa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mandarjoshi/trivia_qa)**, 650K+ trivia question-answer-evidence triples.
- **[Jeopardy-datasets/Jeopardy](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jeopardy-datasets/jeopardy)**, 216,930 real Jeopardy! questions with category and value.
- **[layoric/labeled-multiple-choice-explained](https://huggingface.co/datasets/layoric/labeled-multiple-choice-explained)**, multiple-choice questions with explanations for wrong answers.
- **[under-tree/labeled-multiple-choice](https://huggingface.co/datasets/under-tree/labeled-multiple-choice)**, labeled multiple-choice question set.
- **[truthfulqa/truthful_qa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/truthfulqa/truthful_qa)**, myth-busting questions across 38 categories.
- **[lilacai/lilac-TruthfulQA-MultipleChoice](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lilacai/lilac-TruthfulQA-MultipleChoice)**, TruthfulQA reshaped into clean multiple-choice.
- **[badrex/llm-emoji-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/badrex/llm-emoji-dataset)**, emoji dataset with descriptions and tags.
- **[uberspot/OpenTriviaQA](https://github.com/uberspot/OpenTriviaQA)**, CC-licensed trivia Q&A in a simple text format.

## Tools

- **[LangChain](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain)**, framework for building LLM-powered apps.
