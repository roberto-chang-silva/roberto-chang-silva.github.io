Every so often the group chat runs dry on ideas for game night, and someone inevitably asks "didn't we already play that one?" After digging through recommendations (shoutout to [this r/AskGames thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskGames/comments/t0902p/any_free_party_website_games_that_you_know_of/) for kicking off the list), here's a running collection of browser-based, mostly-free party games that need zero installation, just send a link in Discord/Zoom/whatever and go. Most scale from a handful of people up to a full server.

- **[Gartic Phone](https://garticphone.com/)**, the classic "telephone game but with drawings." Chaos guaranteed once the interpretations start drifting.
- **[Skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/)**, the OG browser Pictionary. Simple, fast rounds, no login required.
- **[Draw Battle](https://drawbattle.io/)**, Skribbl but team-based: two teams of drawers race to guess each other's words before a frantic final round decides the winner.
- **[Pretend You're Xyzzy](https://pyx-1.pretendyoure.xyz/zy/)**, a solid *Cards Against Humanity* clone, no app needed.
- **[All Bad Cards](https://allbad.cards/)**, a cleaner, arguably better take on the same CAH formula. Worth comparing against PYX to see which UI your group prefers.
- **[bad.cards](https://bad.cards/)**, another CAH-style entry if the first two get stale.
- **[Spyfall](https://www.spyfall.app/)**, one player is secretly the "spy" with no idea where the group is; everyone else has to sniff them out through careful questioning without giving the location away. Originally a physical card game from 2014, works great for 3–8 players over voice chat.
- **[Codenames](https://codenames.game/)**, the word-association team game, now free to play online with no signup.
- **[Songlio](https://songl.io/)**, a music-guessing game supporting 2–100 players; everyone picks a song for the group to guess from a 20-second clip, fastest correct guess wins the most points. Great for Zoom calls or if your group has strong music opinions.
- **[TriviaNerd](https://app.trivianerd.com/)**, host and play live multiplayer trivia with custom question sets.
- **[Trivia Royale](https://triviaroyale.io/app/)**, another live multiplayer trivia option worth a look.
- **[Infinite Quiz](https://infinite-quiz.com/en/multiplayer)**, create a private multiplayer quiz session, pick a theme, and invite friends; you can even generate question sets with AI now.
- **[Jigsaw Explorer](https://www.jigsawexplorer.com/)**, sounds tame, but doing a jigsaw puzzle with a big group turns surprisingly chaotic and fun. Good low-key option for winding a game night down.
- **[Storiado: Twisted Party Game](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/storiado-twisted-party-game/id6449257650)**, the one entry here that's an iOS app rather than a browser game, in case your group is fine installing something.
- **[This shared Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/10iOD7Wy_YU4NmkPU7ZH7YTrq11qJAANjZZ0PAotKhR8/mobilebasic)**, a running community list of even more suggestions, worth skimming when this list runs dry too.
