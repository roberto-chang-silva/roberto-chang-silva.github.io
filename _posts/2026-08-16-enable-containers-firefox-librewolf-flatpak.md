For some reason, in my Firefox's installations of my Linux machines the containers function is just not enabled and can't find it in the settings. This includes Librewolf browser as well. It its very simple to activate.

**Step 1:** In the browser's address bar type `about:config` and hit "Accept the risk".

**Step 2:** In the search bar located on top of the site search for `privacy.userContext.enabled` and double click it to set it to `true`

That's it! enjoy Firefox's containers.
